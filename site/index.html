The host

Meet Don Tuco

A toucan has worked the treetops of Bocas longer than anyone has run tours here. Don Tuco is the one who put on a jacket. He greets every guest the way a good innkeeper does, and he never loses the ledger.

  • Answers in the guest's language: Spanish, English, Dutch, and whatever comes next.
  • Checks the calendar before he offers a slot. If 10:00 is taken, he says so and offers 14:00.
  • Writes every reservation in the ledger before he promises it, never after.
  • Says plainly that he's an AI when a guest asks, and that a human reads every booking.
  • Hands the chat to the owner the moment a request leaves his script: changes, complaints, anything that needs a person.
Why this exists
A tour operator's whole business runs on one personal WhatsApp number.

Inquiries arrive at 10 pm, in three languages, while the owner is mid-tour with wet hands. Guests who wait hours for an answer book somewhere else. A website booking widget doesn't fix this, because the guests are already in WhatsApp. Don Tuco lives there too.

How it works

A host, not an autoresponder

Don Tuco runs as an agent with tools. He never invents an answer: availability comes from the calendar, prices from your catalog, and anything he can't resolve goes straight to you.

01

He checks before he promises

Every slot is checked against the booking ledger before it's offered. If 10:00 is taken, he offers 14:00. No double bookings.

02

He closes the booking

Tour, date, group size, name, total price confirmed out loud, then into the ledger. Guests get a confirmation email, you get a notification.

03

He knows when to step aside

Complaints, discounts, groups over your limit, or anything unclear: Don Tuco tells the guest a human will follow up and alerts you immediately. Asked if he's a bot, he says yes.

Under the hood

Built on AWS, end to end

Don Tuco is an entry in the AWS Communication Developer Services Agentic AI Partner Hackathon, built by Divina Endeavors LLC as an AWS partner solution.

  • AWS End User Messaging SocialThe WhatsApp channel itself: inbound guest messages and Don Tuco's replies, including tappable slot lists and location pins.
  • Amazon BedrockClaude runs the agent: the conversation, the tool calls, the judgment on when to escalate.
  • Amazon SESBooking confirmations to guests, notifications and escalations to the owner.
  • DynamoDB + Lambda + SNSThe booking ledger, 24-hour conversation memory, and the event wiring in between.
